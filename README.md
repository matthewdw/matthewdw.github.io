# Online Privacy and Security For Beginners

This guide is written for family, friends and anyone interested in taking small steps to avoid being hacked and take back a little online privacy.

The goal isn't to do everything at once, but to make small incremental changes which will provide you with some protection without feeling like too much of an inconvenience.

## If you're not paying for the product...

There is a saying "*If you're not paying for the product then YOU are the product*".  Free email providers and social media platforms have always used your data and activity to sell to advertisers.  Now your data and photos are being used to train AI.  We will introduce the idea of paying for certain products that you're used to getting for free, such as email.  It's the only way to take back control of your personal data and avoid *being the product*.

## Password Re-use

The single biggest cause of having your accounts hacked.  If you have a favourite password that you use on multiple sites, chances are this password is already available to hackers.  We're going to explain how you can have a ***strong***, ***unique*** password for every single website you use whilst at the same time only ever having to remember one password.
