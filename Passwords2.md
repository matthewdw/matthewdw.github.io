# Password Manager

Go back into Proton Mail and click on the "Pass" button.

<img width="50%" height="50%" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/7b840540-bdb2-4d40-bf97-e99d2ac4f720" />

You'll be asked to enter your Proton Mail password.

Now you're greeted with your empty password vault - ready for you to start filling it with your passwords!  Click "Create Item" -

<img width="3992" height="1094" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/1ab037b5-7b82-43ee-8754-2fcb803b7fa7" />

Fill out the details as they are right now (probably your old Google/whatever email address and your insecure password).  That's fine, we can change them later.  Press "Create item" when you're finished.
