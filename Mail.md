# Email

Starting a slow migration away from whatever free email provider you use at the moment is an excellent place to start.  I suggest Proton Mail - it's end-to-end and zero-access encrypted (meaning no one has access to your emails except you).  It's private, they don't scan your emails to provide targetted adverts and they don't train AI on it.  They can't - only you can access your emails.

You just need to create an account and gradually start using it.  The "Mail Plus" plan is absolutely fine to start with.  Use my link for a 2 week trial and then if you want to keep it, it costs £3.99 a month -

https://account.proton.me/refer-a-friend?referrer=CTFXZ06E&plan=mail2022

It doesn't disrupt what you have at the moment - just start gradually emailing people from your new address and slowly but surely you'll find yourself using your new private email more than your old free one.  Then you can slowly start moving your services like Amazon, EBay etc over to your new address when you have time.

## Choosing a password

When you create your Proton account, pick a strong password.  I recommend a 6 word passphrase which you can generate using their free tool -

https://proton.me/pass/passphrase-generator

Write the password down on a piece of paper and don't lose it.  So in this example we would write down -

*imbecile nature roaming landlady thumb always*

This type of password (a passphrase) is just as secure as one which contains lots of numbers and symbols but is much easier to write down, and perhaps eventually memorise.  When we start storing our passwords in Proton Pass (more on this later) you will see why it is so important that we make sure our Proton account is secure.
